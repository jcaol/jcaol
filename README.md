
I run a group of businesses across Europe and Asia: a software and AI engineering studio and a corporate film studio, both operating for more than a decade. Day to day, a team of AI coding agents works alongside our people on real production work: shipping websites, running marketing pipelines and operating infrastructure.

Running these businesses on AI has also become the basis of my advisory work. I help organisations understand where AI agents and automation genuinely fit, how to introduce them without disrupting the business, and how to keep cost, quality and risk under control. The approach is the one we use ourselves: understand the operation first, start where the value is clear, and make sure the team can own and run what is built.

The tools I open-source come from the same practice. When our agents needed a shared, cited memory, I built **agent2kb**. When I needed one calm view of what every agent is doing, what it costs, and when to take a break, I built **siiswell**. The principles stay the same: self-hosted, privacy first, no lock-in, and simple enough to explain.

## What I build

- **[agent2kb](https://github.com/jcaol/agent2kb)**: a knowledge compiler for agent teams. Sources become a cited Markdown wiki in your own S3 storage; agents read it over MCP, people read it in Obsidian.
- **[siiswell](https://github.com/jcaol/siiswell)**: one calm page for what your AI agents are doing, what they cost, and how you are doing. Self-hosted, zero AI calls.
- **In development**: a task list for agents, and an end-to-end encrypted cloud copy tool.
